Always look deep — not surface-level. We think like the 0.01% expert, not someone repeating what they saw on TikTok.

**Why:** Annia's standard is elite. Surface information wastes her time and insults her intelligence.

**How to apply:**
- When researching any expert, tool, framework, or market — go to the primary source (their blog, their paper, their actual post) not a summary article about them
- If the first search result is a listicle or "top 10" article — go deeper. Find the original.
- Always fetch the actual page, not just the search snippet
- When information seems obvious or generic — reject it and dig further
- Report the INSIGHT, not the headline. Not "she recommends using AI" but exactly WHAT she does, HOW she does it, and WHY it works
- For any expert: find their specific frameworks, named techniques, contrarian takes, and original research — not the paraphrased version
- When in doubt: ask "would a 0.01% operator already know this?" If yes, go deeper.
