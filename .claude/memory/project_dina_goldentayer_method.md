# Dina Goldentayer Method — Adapted for Annia Santana

**Why:** Dina Goldentayer is the #1 agent in Florida AND the US (Douglas Elliman, 3 years running). $735M sold in 2024. $3B+ since 2021. Her methods are the blueprint Annia is modeling.

---

## The 5 Core Methods

### 1. "You're Not Hiring an Agent — You're Hiring a Marketing Agent"
Every listing gets a 100-step marketing plan. Real estate IS a marketing business.
**Annia's version:** Build a 20-step listing marketing checklist and present it in every seller pitch.

### 2. Out-of-Market Reach
Ran billboards in Silicon Valley, Boston, LA, Hamptons, Toronto → pointing buyers to Miami.
**Annia's version:** Run Facebook/Instagram ads targeting Puerto Rican and Venezuelan buyers relocating to Central FL. They are her out-of-market buyer pool. Bilingual = unfair advantage.

### 3. Hyper-Geographic Domination (Own a ZIP Code)
Picked ONE neighborhood (Venetian Islands) and closed more deals there than any agent in history.
**Annia's version:** Pick Deltona OR Orange City → become THE broker with the most sales in that ZIP. Blog it, post it, use it in every pitch: "I've sold more homes in [ZIP] than any other agent."

### 4. Ultra-Selectivity
Only takes "best in class" listings. No average properties.
**Annia's version:** As brand grows, raise minimum listing price. Selectivity = brand equity = higher avg commission.

### 5. Social Media as Core Business Tool
Named "Social Media Influencer of the Year" in real estate. Instagram-driven pipeline.
**Annia's version:** Post consistently on Instagram/Facebook — market stats, listings, bilingual tips, client wins. Every post = free billboard.
