# Annia's Elite Real Estate Prompt Library

## MARKET & DEAL ANALYSIS

### Hyper-Local Market Forecast
Analyze current trends in Deltona/Orlando/Volusia/Seminole Counties: inventory, DOM, median prices,
rent growth, migration, insurance impacts, new developments. Forecast 2026-2028 for SFR/multifamily/BTR.
Identify 3 undervalued submarkets. Probabilities for rate drops. Risks (recession, storms).
Suggest 3 plays for agent/investor with $X budget.

### Property/Deal Underwriting
Institutional-grade analysis: address/details, ARV, rents, expenses, cap rate.
Model: cash flow, IRR, NOI, capex reserves, breakeven occupancy.
Scenario test: rates 5.5%/6.5%/7.5%, 10% vacancy spike, insurance hike.
Buy/hold/flip/renovate/pass? Creative structuring (seller finance, JV).
Florida-specific: homestead exemption, 1031, flood zones.

### CMA Interpreter
Translate MLS/Comps → client-friendly insights.
Client type: buyer/seller/investor/first-time.
3 key trends, pricing/timing implications, emotional analogies.
Negotiation scripts + counter-offer ranges.

Themes: market updates, neighborhood spotlights, buyer tips, investment hacks.
30 captions/hooks + video ideas. Tone: authentic, value-first, local expert.

Simulate negotiation for [deal details].

Win-win framing, creative concessions.
Best/worst/most likely outcomes.
