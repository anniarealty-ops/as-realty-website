## Lead Tool Audit (March 20, 2026)

Reviewed a ChatGPT-generated lead gen tool list from September 2025 against Annia's actual situation as a solo broker.

### Delete — Redundant or Too Expensive
- **BoomTown** — enterprise pricing, built for teams, not solo broker
- **RealGeeks** — same problem, high cost, team-oriented
- **Zurple** — behavioral email automation, not worth the cost at her volume
- **SmartZip** — predictive analytics tool, too expensive, not proven for her market

### Keep on Radar (Not Yet)
- **Zillow Premier Agent** — buyers only, revisit in Month 3+ once listings are flowing

### Real Priority Now (Missing from Old List)
These are the actual highest-ROI channels for Annia in 2026:
1. **Google LSA (Local Services Ads)** — pay-per-lead, shows above all search results, verified badge
2. **Facebook Lead Ads** — direct seller/buyer capture form, great for bilingual targeting
3. **TikTok / Instagram Reels** — organic reach for Vivir Bonito brand, bilingual content

**Why:** Old list was generic national advice. Annia's competitive advantage is bilingual + local — social and Google LSA exploit that directly. MLS-syndication tools don't.

**How to apply:** When Annia asks about lead gen tools or marketing spend, lead with Google LSA + Facebook Lead Ads + Reels. Don't recommend the deleted tools above.
