# /open-house — Open House System (Sign-In + Follow-Up)

## Purpose
Generate everything needed for a successful open house — from sign-in sheet to same-day follow-up texts to 7-day follow-up sequence. Turns open house visitors into leads.

## Trigger
User says "/open-house", "open house follow-up", "I had an open house", or gives a property address + visitor info

## Two Modes

### MODE A — Before the Open House (Prep)
Generate:
1. Sign-in sheet template (Name / Phone / Email / Are you working with an agent? / Timeline / Pre-approved?)
2. Door hanger script for neighbors (invite them + ask if they know anyone buying)
3. Social media post announcing the open house (English + Spanish)
4. Talking points for Annia during the open house

### MODE B — After the Open House (Follow-Up)
Ask: "How many visitors? Paste the sign-in info and tell me who seemed most interested."

Then generate follow-up for each category:

**HOT — Interested, no agent, pre-approved:**
- Text within 2 hours: personal, reference something specific from conversation
- Email same day: full property info + offer to schedule private showing
- Day 3 follow-up if no response

**WARM — Interested but has agent / not ready:**
- Text next day: friendly, low pressure
- Add to nurture list
- Day 7 check-in

**NEIGHBOR — Came from neighborhood:**
- Text or call: "Thank you for stopping by — do you know anyone looking to buy in the area?"
- Ask about their own home: "Have you thought about what yours might be worth?"

**NO CONTACT — Left without signing in:**
- Door hanger follow-up if address is known
- Social retargeting note

### Standard Follow-Up Texts (Ready to Copy/Paste)

**Same day (HOT lead):**
"Hi [Name], it was great meeting you at [Address] today! I'd love to schedule a private showing for you. When works best? — Annia (407) 492-1114"

**Day 1 (WARM lead):**
"Hi [Name], thanks for stopping by [Address]! Let me know if you have any questions or want to see other homes in the area. — Annia (407) 492-1114"

**Neighbor:**
"Hi [Name], so glad you came by today! If you ever want to know what your home is worth, I'd be happy to run a free analysis. — Annia (407) 492-1114"

## After Delivering
Ask: "Want me to build out the full 7-day follow-up sequence for your hot leads from today?"
