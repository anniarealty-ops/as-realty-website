# /listing-presentation — Full Listing Presentation Builder

## Purpose
Build a complete, professional listing presentation for Annia to win a listing appointment. From a single address, generate everything needed to walk in and close the seller.

## Trigger
User says "/listing-presentation", "listing appointment", "build my presentation", or gives an address + appointment date

## What to Collect
1. Property address
2. Seller's name(s)
3. Appointment date/time
4. What Annia knows about the seller's situation (motivation, timeline, price expectations)
5. Any competing agents she knows about?

## Full Presentation Output

### SECTION 1 — Pre-Appointment Research
- Property details (pull from Volusia/Orange County appraiser)
- Estimated value range (quick CMA)
- Days on market for similar homes in the area
- List-to-sale price ratio for the neighborhood
- How long the seller has owned it (equity estimate)

### SECTION 2 — Opening (The Hook)
Script for the first 60 seconds:
- Acknowledge their goal
- Position Annia as the local expert
- Set the agenda for the meeting
- Build rapport — bilingual if seller is Spanish-speaking

### SECTION 3 — About AS Realty & Annia
- Licensed Broker Owner (not just an agent — she IS the broker)
- Local Central FL expert: Volusia, Orange, Osceola
- Bilingual English/Spanish — reaches more buyers
- 27 transactions, $194K+ in commissions, 9 five-star Google reviews
- Personal, not corporate — direct access to the decision-maker

### SECTION 4 — Marketing Plan (What Makes Annia Different)
- MLS + Zillow + Realtor.com + Homes.com syndication
- Professional photos (mention if included)
- Social media: Instagram, Facebook, Spanish-language content (Vivir Bonito)
- SMS/text marketing to active buyer list
- Open house strategy
- Bilingual buyer outreach

### SECTION 5 — Pricing Strategy
- Present the CMA results (3 comps)
- Recommended price range with reasoning
- The cost of overpricing (days on market = lower offers)
- Net sheet at recommended price

### SECTION 6 — Objection Responses
Pre-built answers for:
- "I want to list higher and see what happens"
- "Another agent said they could get me more"
- "I'm going to try FSBO first"
- "Your commission is too high"
- "I need to think about it"

### SECTION 7 — Close
Script for asking for the listing agreement:
- Trial close question
- Handle final objection
- Next steps if they say yes (photos, sign, go live)

## Tone
Warm, confident, data-backed. Not pushy. Annia is the expert helping them make a smart decision — not a salesperson.

## After Delivering
Ask: "Want me to generate the actual net sheet for this appointment, or create a leave-behind one-pager for the seller to keep?"
