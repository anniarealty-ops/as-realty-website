# /today — Daily Operations Command for Annia Santana

## Purpose
Generate Annia's complete daily plan optimized for $15K/month income goal. Pulls together her active leads, follow-up priorities, content tasks, and business admin into one structured, time-blocked day.

## Trigger
User types `/today` or says "give me my day" or "plan my day"

## What to Output

### 1. Today's Top 3 Revenue-Generating Actions
Identify the 3 tasks most likely to move a deal forward or generate income today. Always check:
- Any active hot leads that need follow-up (Renee Manzo, Yanett Bello, or any mentioned in conversation)
- Any pending closings or CDA documents needed
- Any SimpleTexting replies to respond to
- Any expired listing opportunities to pursue

### 2. Time-Blocked Schedule (8 AM – 6 PM)
Use Pomodoro blocks (25 min work / 5 min break). Structure:
- **8:00–9:00 AM** — Revenue hour: lead follow-up, SMS replies, listing appointments
- **9:00–10:00 AM** — Prospecting: expired listings, investor outreach, title company calls
- **10:00–11:30 AM** — Deep work: CMA analysis, listing prep, content creation
- **11:30 AM–12:00 PM** — Admin: email, GHL updates, paperwork
- **12:00–1:00 PM** — Lunch + Spanish-language content recording (Vivir Bonito)
- **1:00–3:00 PM** — Client-facing: showings, calls, consultations
- **3:00–4:00 PM** — Follow-ups + pipeline updates
- **4:00–5:00 PM** — Learning + strategic planning (Teresa Torres / Sid Saladi content)
- **5:00–6:00 PM** — Wrap: tomorrow's prep, HHA tasks for father

### 3. Pipeline Tracker Prompt
Ask: "Any leads to update today? Tell me: Name / Status (Hot-Warm-Cold) / Last Contact / Next Action"
Then format as a clean table.

### 4. Content Task (10 min max)
One micro-content task: Instagram caption, Google Business post, or SMS follow-up copy

### 5. Affirmation + Focus Word
One elite-level mindset anchor for the day. No fluff — make it strategic and real.

## Behavior Rules
- Always ask "What's already on your plate today?" before generating the full plan
- If she mentions a specific lead or deal, prioritize that in the schedule
- Keep the plan realistic — she is also an HHA caregiver, schedule must have flexibility
- Always end with: "What do you want to tackle first?"
