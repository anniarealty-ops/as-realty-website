# /content-repurposing — Bilingual Content Repurposing Engine

## Purpose
Take ONE piece of content (video transcript, voice memo, blog post, or raw idea) and turn it into a full bilingual content suite for AS Realty / Vivir Bonito brand.

## Trigger
User says "/content-repurposing", "repurpose this", "turn this into content", "make social posts from this", or pastes a transcript

## Input Types Accepted
- YouTube video transcript (paste it)
- Voice memo transcription (paste it)
- Blog post or article
- Raw idea or bullet points ("I want to talk about...")
- An existing Instagram post to expand

## Output Suite (always produce all 6)

### 1. Blog Post (English)
- 400–600 words
- SEO-friendly headline
- 3–4 sections with subheadings
- Call to action: "Call/text Annia at (407) 492-1114" or link to asrealtyfl.com
- Style: warm, expert, local Florida flavor

### 2. Blog Post (Spanish)
- Same structure, fully translated — not just Google Translate
- Natural, conversational Spanish (not formal Castilian — use Latin American style)
- CTA in Spanish: "Llama o escríbele a Annia al (407) 492-1114"

### 3. Instagram Caption (English)
- 150–200 words
- Hook on line 1 (no "Hey guys!" — start with a bold fact or question)
- 3–5 value points
- CTA: follow, DM, or call
- 10 relevant hashtags (Central FL real estate focused)

### 4. Instagram Caption (Spanish)
- Same structure, fully in Spanish
- Hashtags: mix of English + Spanish real estate tags
- End with: "¡Hablo español! 📞 (407) 492-1114"

### 5. SimpleTexting SMS (English)
- Max 160 characters
- Direct, no fluff
- Personalization token: "Hi {firstName},"
- End with: "(407) 492-1114"

### 6. SimpleTexting SMS (Spanish)
- Max 160 characters
- "Hola {firstName},"
- End with: "Hablo español. (407) 492-1114"

## Brand Voice Rules
- Vivir Bonito tone: warm, bilingual, real, not salesy
- AS Realty tone: professional, local expert, data-backed
- Never use: "Hey guys!", "So excited to share!", "I'm blessed to announce"
- Always use: specific local references (Deltona, Orange City, Volusia County, Central FL)
- Always include: one real data point or market fact when possible

## After Delivering the Suite
Ask: "Want me to also create a YouTube description, a Google Business post, or a follow-up email sequence from this content?"
