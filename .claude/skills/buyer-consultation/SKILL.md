# /buyer-consultation — Buyer Consultation Framework

## Purpose
Run a structured buyer consultation that qualifies the buyer, sets expectations, and positions Annia as the expert guide — not just a door-opener. Covers financing, timeline, needs/wants, and the buyer agency agreement.

## Trigger
User says "/buyer-consultation", "buyer consult", "new buyer", or describes a new buyer lead

## What to Collect
1. Buyer name(s)
2. How they found Annia (referral, expired listing, website, social)
3. What they've told her so far (budget, area, timeline)
4. Are they pre-approved? With whom?
5. First-time buyer?
6. Renting now? Lease end date?

## Full Consultation Output

### SECTION 1 — Qualifying Questions (Ask in This Order)
**Financial:**
- Are you pre-approved or pre-qualified? (Different things — explain)
- What's your target monthly payment or purchase price?
- How much are you putting down?
- Any large deposits or cash gifts coming? (Affects underwriting)

**Timeline:**
- When do you need to be in a home?
- Any flexibility on that date?
- Are you locked into a lease? When does it end?

**Needs vs. Wants:**
- What's your non-negotiable list? (beds, baths, garage, pool)
- What's your nice-to-have list?
- What areas are you open to? Any you won't consider?
- Schools important?

**Motivation:**
- Why are you moving now?
- What happens if you don't find something in your timeline?
- Have you been looking long? Lost any offers?

### SECTION 2 — Education Script
What to explain to every buyer:
1. How the buying process works (pre-approval → search → offer → inspection → closing)
2. Current Central FL market (competition, days on market, over-asking offers)
3. What Annia does for them (negotiate, protect, guide — not just show houses)
4. Buyer agency agreement — explain it clearly, not defensively
5. What it costs them (typically nothing — seller pays)

### SECTION 3 — Red Flags to Note
- Not pre-approved (refer to lender before showing homes)
- Unrealistic price vs. wants (educate with comps)
- Looking with multiple agents (address this directly)
- Timeline mismatch (wants to close in 2 weeks with no pre-approval)

### SECTION 4 — Lender Referral Script
If not pre-approved:
"Before we start touring homes, let's get you pre-approved — it's free, takes about 20 minutes, and makes your offers much stronger. I work with a great lender who's very responsive. Want me to connect you?"

### SECTION 5 — Next Steps Script
After consultation:
- If qualified → set up MLS search, schedule showings
- If not ready → nurture plan (follow up in 30/60/90 days)
- Send follow-up text within 1 hour of the meeting

## After Delivering
Ask: "Want me to draft the follow-up text to send this buyer right now, or set up a nurture sequence if they're not ready yet?"
