# /frame — Content Multiplier Skill
## Ethan Mollick's "Frame Translation" — one input → multiple audience versions

## Purpose
Take ONE piece of content and instantly transform it into every format Annia needs.
Saves writing the same thing 5 different ways from scratch.

## How to invoke
```
/frame [paste your content or describe what you have]
```

Examples:
- `/frame 3/2 Deltona home, 1,450 sqft, updated kitchen, pool, $285K`
- `/frame new listing in Orange City, 4/2, corner lot, 2-car garage`
- `/frame my seller Renee wants to net $430K, home needs minor repairs`
- `/frame tip: Central FL buyers can still negotiate 2-3% off asking price in 2026`

---

## Execution Steps

### Step 1 — Identify what you have
Determine if input is:
- A **property** (listing) → produce all real estate formats
- A **tip/advice** (content) → produce all social formats
- A **client situation** → produce all outreach formats

### Step 2 — Ask ONE clarifying question if needed
Only ask if the input is missing a critical detail (price, location, or goal).

### Step 3 — Produce ALL formats below

---

## FOR A PROPERTY LISTING:

#### 1. MLS DESCRIPTION (250 words max)
Professional, factual, keyword-rich. No banned words. Highlight top 3 features.

#### 2. SELLER COVER LETTER (to give to the homeowner)
Warm, personal. "Here's how I'll market your home." 150 words. Builds trust.

#### 3. INSTAGRAM CAPTION — English (bilingual brand)
Hook in first line. Conversational. 200 words max. CTA + hashtags.

#### 4. INSTAGRAM CAPTION — Spanish
Full translation. Warm, not robotic.

#### 5. SMS BLAST (160 chars max)
Direct. For expired leads or investor list.
Example: "3/2 pool home in Deltona, $285K. Investors welcome. Reply YES for details. -Annia (407)492-1114"

#### 6. EMAIL SUBJECT LINE (3 options)
Short, curiosity-driven. No clickbait.

#### 7. FACEBOOK INVESTOR POST
Teaser only — NO address, NO price. Goal: get DMs.
Example: "Off-market deal alert — Central FL 3/2, updated, priced below market. Serious investors only. DM me."

---

## FOR A TIP / CONTENT IDEA:

#### 1. INSTAGRAM CAPTION — English (bilingual)
Hook + tip + CTA. 200 words. Hashtags.

#### 2. INSTAGRAM CAPTION — Spanish

#### 3. FACEBOOK POST — English (longer, more educational)

#### 4. FACEBOOK POST — Spanish

#### 5. SMS TIP (for subscriber list, 160 chars)

#### 6. EMAIL NEWSLETTER PARAGRAPH (100 words)

#### 2. FOLLOW-UP EMAIL (subject + body)
#### 3. VOICEMAIL SCRIPT (30 seconds)
#### 4. OBJECTION HANDLER (for most likely pushback)
