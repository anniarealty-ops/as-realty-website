# /lo-today — Daily Operations Command for a Loan Officer

## Purpose
Generate a complete daily plan optimized for a loan officer's pipeline — from new leads to active files to referral partner relationships. Keeps closings on track and pipeline full.

## Trigger
User types `/lo-today` or "plan my day" or "give me my day"

## What to Ask First
"What's already locked in today? Any closings, appraisals, rate locks expiring, or partner calls scheduled?"

## Output Structure

### 1. Today's Top 3 Revenue Actions
Always check:
- Any files with rate locks expiring in 7 days or less (URGENT)
- Any loans waiting on borrower conditions (income docs, bank statements, ID)
- Any new leads from Realtor referrals to call within the hour
- Any pre-approvals to finalize for active buyers

### 2. Time-Blocked Schedule (8 AM – 6 PM)
- **8:00–8:30 AM** — Review pipeline: check all active files, flag any issues
- **8:30–9:00 AM** — Call new leads (first 1 hour = highest contact rate)
- **9:00–10:00 AM** — Referral partner touch base: call/text 3 Realtors or builders
- **10:00–11:30 AM** — File processing: review conditions, request docs, update LOS
- **11:30 AM–12:00 PM** — Follow up on pending conditions + underwriter communication
- **12:00–1:00 PM** — Lunch + send 1 market update email to referral partner list
- **1:00–2:00 PM** — Pre-qualification calls: new applicants, credit pulls, scenario analysis
- **2:00–3:30 PM** — Processor/underwriter communication: push files forward
- **3:30–4:00 PM** — Borrower status updates (everyone deserves a weekly call)
- **4:00–5:00 PM** — Referral partner CRM update + thank you notes/texts
- **5:00–5:30 PM** — Tomorrow prep: identify 3 files that need action first thing

### 3. Pipeline Tracker Prompt
Ask: "Give me your active files: Borrower Name / Stage (App/Processing/UW/CTC/Closing) / Rate Lock Expiration / Blocker"
Format as a table and flag any rate locks within 14 days in ALL CAPS.

### 4. Daily Content Task (10 min)
One of:
- Rate update post for Instagram/Facebook ("Rates moved today — here's what it means for buyers")
- Realtor tip text ("Quick tip for your buyers this week...")
- Google Business post on a loan product (FHA, VA, DSCR, conventional)

### 5. Focus Word
One elite mindset anchor. Pipeline is built in phone calls that feel uncomfortable. Make the call.

## Rules
- Rate lock expirations are the #1 priority — always surface these first
- Every borrower should get a proactive status call at least once a week
- Referral partners (Realtors) must hear from her at least 3x per week
- Always end with: "What do you want to tackle first?"
