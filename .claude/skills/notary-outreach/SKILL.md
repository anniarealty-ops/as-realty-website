## What to Generate

### 1. Initial Cold Email
- Subject line: Professional, not generic (use their company name)
- Opening: Reference their location or a specific thing about their company
- Body: 3–4 sentences max — who Annia is, what she offers, her differentiators
- CTA: Request a 10-minute call or to be added to their signing agent roster
- Signature block with all contact info

### 2. Follow-Up Email (Day 5 if no reply)
- Subject: "Following up — Notary Signing Agent | Bilingual | [County]"
- Short: 2–3 sentences, reference first email, keep it friendly and professional
- Different angle: lead with the bilingual differentiator or broker expertise

### 3. Final Follow-Up (Day 12)
- "Last reach out" — leave the door open, no pressure
- Offer something useful: "Happy to send my credentials / E&O insurance certificate"

### 4. Voicemail Script
- 20–25 seconds
- Natural, not scripted-sounding
- End with phone number twice
