# /lo-borrower-outreach — Borrower Communication Templates

## Purpose
Generate professional, warm borrower communication for every stage of the loan process — from first contact to closing day. Keeps borrowers informed, reduces anxiety, and eliminates the "what's going on with my loan?" calls.

## Trigger
User says "/lo-borrower-outreach", "draft a borrower message", "what do I say to my borrower", or describes a loan stage

## Loan Stage Templates (always ask which stage first)

### Stage 1: New Lead — First Contact
- Within 1 hour of inquiry
- Warm, fast, direct
- Confirm what they're looking for, next step is phone call
- Text version (160 char) + email version

### Stage 2: Application Submitted
- Confirm receipt, set timeline expectations
- List exactly what docs you need from them
- Tell them what happens next (processing → underwriting → CTC → closing)

### Stage 3: Conditions Request
- Clear, numbered list of what's needed
- Explain WHY each document is needed (reduces pushback)
- Give a deadline
- Offer to help them find the document if they're confused

### Stage 4: Underwriting Update (weekly check-in)
- Proactive status update even if nothing changed
- "No news is good news — here's where things stand"
- Reduces anxiety, builds trust

### Stage 5: Clear to Close (CTC)
- Celebratory but professional
- Tell them what closing day looks like
- What to bring, what NOT to do (no new credit, no big purchases)
- Wire fraud warning (mandatory)

### Stage 6: Closing Day
- Morning of closing: quick text with time, location, what to bring
- Amount needed to close (wired or cashier's check)

### Stage 7: Post-Closing Follow-Up (Day 7)
- Thank you + ask for Google review
- Ask for referrals
- Set expectation for when first payment is due

### Stage 8: Annual Mortgage Check-In
- Happy anniversary of their home purchase
- Quick rate check offer
- Referral ask

## Format Rules
- Always offer BOTH text (SMS-length) AND email versions
- Keep email under 150 words — borrowers don't read long emails
- Never use jargon without explaining it
- Always end with one clear next step
- Sign off with name, phone, NMLS number placeholder

## After Delivering
Ask: "Want me to also create a follow-up sequence if they don't respond?"
