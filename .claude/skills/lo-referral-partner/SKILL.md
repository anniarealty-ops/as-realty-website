# /lo-referral-partner — Realtor & Builder Referral Partner Outreach

## Purpose
Generate outreach scripts, value-add content, and relationship-building communication for loan officers targeting Realtor and builder referral partners. The #1 source of purchase loan volume is Realtor relationships — this skill systematizes that.

## Trigger
User says "/lo-referral-partner", "Realtor outreach", "referral partner message", "how do I get more Realtor referrals", or "partner communication"

## What to Ask First
"Who are you reaching out to? (New Realtor / Existing partner / Builder / Open house drop-in) and what's the goal — first intro, staying top of mind, or following up on a referral?"

## Output Templates

### 1. Cold Intro to a New Realtor
- Text version (short, personal, not salesy)
- Email version (professional, mentions a specific value prop)
- Voicemail script (20 seconds max)
- Key angle: speed of pre-approval, communication style, problem-solving ability

### 2. Weekly Value-Add Touch (Existing Partners)
Send every week — rotate through these:
- **Rate update:** "Rates moved this week — here's what it means for your buyers"
- **Market insight:** local stats (days on market, median price, inventory)
- **Program spotlight:** FHA, VA, DSCR, down payment assistance
- **Success story:** closed a tough loan — brief story of how you solved the problem
- **Client spotlight:** "Your buyer [first name] just closed! They said [quote]"

### 3. Open House Drop-In Follow-Up
- Text within 2 hours of visiting an open house
- Short, friendly, reference something specific from the conversation
- Offer: pre-approval letters same day, fast communication

### 4. After Sending a Referral
- Text to thank the Realtor for the referral
- Update when borrower applies
- Update at CTC
- Update at closing with a referral fee summary if applicable

### 5. Reactivation (Partner Who Went Quiet)
- 30-day no-contact reconnect
- Light touch, no pressure
- Lead with value (rate update or market stat), not a sales pitch

### 6. Closing Gift + Google Review Request
- Post-closing text to Realtor
- Thank them for the partnership
- Ask for a Google review
- Soft referral ask

## Content Calendar for Referral Partners (Weekly)
| Day | Touch Type | Format |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| Monday | Rate update | Text |
| Wednesday | Market stat or tip | Email or Social share |
| Friday | Personal check-in or deal story | Text |

## Rules
- Never pitch on the first touch — lead with value
- Always reference something personal or specific (their listing, their market)
- Keep all texts under 160 characters
- Frequency: minimum 3 touches per week for top 10 partners

## After Delivering
Ask: "Want me to build a 30-day referral partner nurture calendar for a specific agent or team?"
