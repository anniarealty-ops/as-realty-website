name: real-estate-analysis
description: Analyze a real estate deal, listing, or property for Annia. Use when evaluating a property, running numbers, or reviewing a potential listing.
---

When Annia provides a property or deal, analyze it using:

1. **ARV vs Purchase Price** — is there equity on day 1?
2. **Gross Commission Estimate** — at 3% buyer side or 6% total
3. **Comparable Sales** — ask for 3 comps if not provided
4. **Red Flags** — flood zone, insurance, HOA, age of roof/AC
5. **Action Recommendation** — List it, pass, or negotiate? Why?

Always give a clear YES/NO/NEGOTIATE verdict with reasoning.
Format output: sections with bold headers, bullet points, a final verdict box.
