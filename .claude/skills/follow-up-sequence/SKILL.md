# /follow-up-sequence — 7-Day Lead Follow-Up System

## Purpose
Generate a complete 7-day multi-touch follow-up sequence for any lead type. Never let a lead go cold again. Covers SMS, email, voicemail, and social touch — all pre-written and ready to send.

## Trigger
User says "/follow-up-sequence", "follow-up plan", "lead went cold", "what do I say next", or describes a lead situation

## What to Ask First
"Tell me about this lead: (1) Where did they come from? (2) What did they say or do? (3) What's their situation — buyer, seller, investor, or unknown? (4) When was the last contact?"

## Lead Types (customize sequence for each)

### TYPE A — Expired Listing (Motivated Seller)
- Day 1 (Hour 1): First SMS — direct, solution-focused
- Day 1 (Hour 4): Voicemail if no reply
- Day 2: Email with market data + net sheet offer
- Day 3: SMS — different angle (bilingual offer or investor option)
- Day 5: Value-add touch (market update or neighborhood sale)
- Day 7: Final SMS — "last reach out" with soft close
- Day 14: Monthly check-in added to CRM

### TYPE B — Open House Visitor
- Day 0 (same day): Warm thank-you text
- Day 1: Email with full property details + alternatives
- Day 3: SMS — check in, any questions?
- Day 5: New listing alert (similar homes)
- Day 7: "Still looking?" check-in
- Day 14: Monthly market update

### TYPE C — Website/Lead Magnet Lead
- Day 0 (within 5 min): Auto-response SMS (from GHL)
- Day 1: Personal follow-up call + voicemail
- Day 2: Email with free guide delivered
- Day 4: SMS — "Did you get a chance to read it?"
- Day 6: Value email (market update for their area)
- Day 8: Final personal touch — offer free consultation

### TYPE D — Cold/Referral Lead
- Day 1: Warm intro text (mention who referred them)
- Day 2: Email with bio + what you do
- Day 4: SMS — "Any questions about the market?"
- Day 7: Value touch — relevant article or stat
- Day 14: Check-in + offer

## Full 7-Day Sequence Output Format
For each touch, generate:
- **Medium:** SMS / Email / Voicemail / Social
- **Timing:** Day X, Hour X
- **Message:** Full copy, ready to send
- **Personalization tokens:** {firstName}, {address}, {area}
- **Goal:** What response you're looking for

## Rules
- SMS: max 160 characters, always include name + phone
- Email: max 150 words, one clear CTA
- Never send more than 1 touch per day
- Always offer value before asking for the meeting
- Day 7 is always the "last reach out" — no more after that (respect boundaries)
- Bilingual: always offer Spanish version of each touch

Ask: "Want me to load this sequence into a SimpleTexting campaign, or build a GHL automation workflow from this?"
