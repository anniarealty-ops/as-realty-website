# /post — Vivir Bonito Content Skill

## Purpose
Creates bilingual (English + Spanish) social media content for the Vivir Bonito brand.
Annia Santana | AS Realty & Investments | Central Florida

## How to invoke
```
/post [topic or idea]
```
Examples:
- `/post why Central Florida buyers are still winning in 2026`
- `/post tips for selling your home fast in Deltona`
- `/post what to know before buying in Kissimmee`
- `/post expired listing — your home can still sell`

---

## Execution Steps

### Step 1 — Clarify (if topic is vague)
Ask ONE question only:
> "Is this for a buyer tip, seller tip, investor insight, or lifestyle/community post?"

If topic is clear, skip to Step 2.

### Step 2 — Research (if needed)
If the topic involves market stats, rates, or current events — web-search first before writing. Never invent numbers.

### Step 3 — Write the content

Produce ALL of the following outputs:

---

#### INSTAGRAM CAPTION (English)
- 150–300 words
- Hook in first line (no "I" as first word)
- Warm, conversational, expert tone
- Short paragraphs (2-3 lines max)
- No em dashes. Use periods or commas.
- No banned words: just, very, really, literally, actually, basically, delve, embark, game-changer, unlock, revolutionize, utilize, seamless, robust, cutting-edge
- End with a soft CTA: "Questions? DM me or call (407) 492-1114 | asrealtyfl.com"
- Hashtags (always include):
  #VivirBonito #CentralFlorida #FloridaRealEstate #AnniaSantana #ASRealty #OrlandoRealEstate #DeltonaFL #HomeBuyingTips #HomeSelling #BienesRaicesFlorida

#### INSTAGRAM CAPTION (Spanish)
- Full translation of English caption
- Keep same tone — conversational, warm, NOT a word-for-word robot translation
- Add: #CasasEnFlorida #ComprarCasa #VenderCasa #FloridaLatino

#### FACEBOOK POST (English)
- Longer version (300–500 words)
- More educational, add 1–2 extra details or stats
- Same CTA and hashtags as IG

#### FACEBOOK POST (Spanish)
- Full translation of the Facebook English post

#### YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION (if topic fits a video)
- 100-150 words
- SEO-optimized for Central Florida real estate keywords
- Include: "Subscribe | Follow @VivirBonito | (407) 492-1114 | asrealtyfl.com"
- Only produce this if the topic clearly fits a video format. Skip otherwise.

---

### Step 4 — Quality Check (self-review before presenting)
Before presenting output, verify:
- [ ] No em dashes in any post
- [ ] No banned words used
- [ ] Both English and Spanish versions present
- [ ] CTA includes phone number and website
- [ ] All required hashtags included
- [ ] No invented statistics (if numbers used, they came from web search)

### Step 5 — Present to Annia
Format output clearly with headers:
```
## INSTAGRAM — English
[caption]

## INSTAGRAM — Spanish
[caption]

## FACEBOOK — English
[post]

## FACEBOOK — Spanish
[post]

## YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION (if applicable)
[description]
```

End with:
> "Ready to copy-paste. Want me to adjust tone, length, or add a specific stat?"
