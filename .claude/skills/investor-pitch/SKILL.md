# /investor-pitch — Off-Market Deal Investor Pitch

## Purpose
Generate teaser posts, DM scripts, and finder's fee pitch for off-market deals. Built for Annia's Facebook group strategy — post the deal without the address, get DMs, collect finder's fee before making the introduction.

## Trigger
User says "/investor-pitch", "investor deal", "off-market pitch", "Facebook group post", or gives property details

## Annia's Model
- Post teaser (no address, no price) in Central FL investor Facebook groups
- Interested investors DM her
- She qualifies them: cash or hard money? Proof of funds?
- Charges finder's fee: $3,000–$5,000 BEFORE making the introduction
- Then connects investor to seller

## What to Collect
1. Property location (city/area only — no address in post)
2. Property type (SFH, duplex, multifamily)
3. Bed/bath/sqft (approximate)
4. Condition (turnkey, light rehab, full rehab)
5. Asking price or ARV
6. Why it's a deal (equity, below market, distressed seller)

## Output

### 1. Facebook Group Teaser Post
- No address, no seller info
- Create urgency and intrigue
- Highlight the investment angle (equity, below market, cash flow)
- End with: "DM me for details — serious investors only, POF required"
- English version + Spanish version

Example format:
"🏠 OFF-MARKET DEAL ALERT — Deltona area
3/2 SFH | Light rehab needed
ARV: $XXX,XXX | Asking well below market
Seller motivated, looking for quick close
NOT on the MLS — you won't find this anywhere else
DM me if you're a cash buyer or have hard money ready. Proof of funds required before I share details. — Annia (407) 492-1114"

### 2. DM Response Script (When Investors Reach Out)
Step 1 — Qualify:
"Thanks for reaching out! Before I share details, a few quick questions: Are you paying cash or using hard money? What's your typical purchase price range? And can you provide proof of funds or a bank statement?"

Step 2 — Finder's Fee Disclosure:
"Great — just so you know, I charge a finder's fee of $[3,000–5,000] to connect you with the seller. This is paid before the introduction. Does that work for you?"

Step 3 — If They Agree:
Send a simple finder's fee agreement (one page), collect payment or commitment, then make the introduction.

### 3. Finder's Fee Agreement (Simple)
Generate a one-paragraph agreement:
- Annia's name + AS Realty
- Investor's name
- Property description (no address yet)
- Finder's fee amount
- Payment terms (due before introduction)
- Both signatures

### 4. Target Facebook Groups (Central FL)
- Central FL Real Estate Investors
- Orlando Real Estate Investors
- Wholesale Real Estate Florida
- REIA (Real Estate Investors Association) groups
- Deltona/Volusia County investor groups

## Rules
- NEVER post the address publicly
- ALWAYS collect finder's fee BEFORE the introduction
- ALWAYS get proof of funds before sharing property details
- Position as exclusive and scarce — "serious investors only"

## After Delivering
Ask: "Want me to draft the finder's fee agreement as a PDF, or create a follow-up message for investors who went quiet?"
