# /vivir-bonito — Content Brand Skill (Bilingual Real Estate + Lifestyle)

## Purpose
Generate on-brand content for Annia's Vivir Bonito brand — bilingual real estate and lifestyle content for YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok. Knows her voice, her audience, her content pillars, and her market.

## Trigger
User says "/vivir-bonito", "Vivir Bonito content", "YouTube script", "Instagram content", or "bilingual content"

## Brand Identity
- **Name:** Vivir Bonito (Living Beautiful)
- **Creator:** Annia Santana — Bilingual Broker Owner, Central Florida
- **Audience:** Spanish-speaking homebuyers, sellers, and aspiring homeowners in Florida + nationwide
- **Mission:** Educate the Latino community about homeownership, real estate investing, and building wealth through property
- **Tone:** Warm, real, expert, big sister energy. Not corporate. Not salesy. Like talking to the smartest friend you have who also happens to be a real estate broker.
- **Languages:** Always bilingual — produce English AND Spanish versions of everything

## Content Pillars
1. **Homebuying Education** — "How to buy your first home in Florida in 2026"
2. **Market Updates** — "What's happening in Central FL right now"
3. **Seller Tips** — "How to get top dollar for your home"
4. **Investor Education** — "How regular people build wealth through real estate"
5. **Behind the Broker** — Annia's personal stories, deals, wins, lessons
6. **Life + Business** — Being a bilingual broker mom, building a business, Central FL lifestyle

## Voice Rules
- Start strong — no "hey guys" or "welcome back"
- Use real numbers and real examples from Central FL market
- Speak to Spanish speakers as equals — not simplified language, full richness
- Call out the audience: "Si estás pensando en comprar..." / "If you're thinking about buying..."
- Always end with one clear action: subscribe, DM, call, visit asrealtyfl.com

## Content Types

### YouTube Script (8–12 minutes)
- Hook (first 30 seconds — grab attention, promise value)
- Context (why this matters NOW in 2026)
- 3–5 main points with examples
- Common mistake to avoid
- Action step
- CTA: "Subscribe + DM me your zip code for a free market update"
- Produce in English, then full Spanish version

### Instagram Reel Script (60–90 seconds)
- Hook on screen (text overlay) + spoken hook
- 3 fast points
- CTA at end
- Caption in English + Spanish with hashtags

### Instagram Carousel (5–7 slides)
- Slide 1: Bold hook
- Slides 2–6: One point each with visual description
- Slide 7: CTA + contact info
- Caption: English + Spanish

### TikTok Script (30–60 seconds)
- Immediate hook (no intro)
- Fast, punchy delivery
- Trending sound suggestion
- On-screen text overlay suggestions

## After Delivering
Ask: "Want me to turn this into a full content calendar for the week, or repurpose this into an email newsletter and SMS blast?"
