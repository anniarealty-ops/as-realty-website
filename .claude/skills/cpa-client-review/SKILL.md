# /cpa-client-review — Client Financial Review & Advisory

## Purpose
Run a structured financial review for a CPA client — analyzing their P&L, balance sheet, tax situation, or any financial data they bring in. Turns raw numbers into clear insights and actionable advice the client can actually understand.

## Trigger
User says "/cpa-client-review", "review these financials", "analyze this P&L", "client review prep", or pastes financial data

## What to Ask First
"What type of review? (Tax planning / P&L analysis / Balance sheet / Cash flow / Full financial review) And what's the client's business type? (W-2 employee / Self-employed / S-Corp / LLC / Partnership)"

## Output

### 1. Financial Health Snapshot
Quick scorecard — Green / Yellow / Red for:
- Revenue trend (growing, flat, declining?)
- Profitability (net margin vs. industry benchmark)
- Cash position (months of runway)
- Tax liability estimate
- Key ratios (current ratio, debt-to-equity if applicable)

### 2. Tax Planning Highlights
Always surface:
- Estimated tax liability for the year
- Opportunities to reduce: retirement contributions, Section 179, home office, vehicle, health insurance deduction
- Quarterly estimated tax recommendation
- Any red flags (income spikes, unreported 1099s, missed deductions)

### 3. Top 3 Advisory Recommendations
Specific, actionable, prioritized:
1. Highest-impact tax savings move
2. Cash flow or business structure improvement
3. Forward-looking planning (next year, retirement, entity structure)

### 4. Client Meeting Talking Points
Plain-English summary of everything above — as if explaining to the client face-to-face. No jargon. Focus on what they need to DO, not accounting theory.

### 5. Questions to Ask the Client
List of follow-up questions to gather missing information before finalizing the return or advisory letter.

### 6. Red Flags Checklist
Common issues to verify:
- [ ] All 1099s accounted for?
- [ ] Business vs. personal expenses clearly separated?
- [ ] Home office properly documented?
- [ ] Vehicle mileage log maintained?
- [ ] Estimated taxes paid on time?
- [ ] Payroll taxes current (if applicable)?

## After Delivering
Ask: "Want me to draft a client letter summarizing these findings, or build a tax savings projection for next year?"
