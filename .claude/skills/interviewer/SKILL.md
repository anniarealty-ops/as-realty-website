# /interviewer — AI Discovery Interviewer
## Teresa Torres' Continuous Discovery + Opportunity Solution Tree method
## Applied to: seller consultations, buyer qualification, investor meetings, lead calls

## Purpose
Before any important meeting or call, build the exact questions to ask.
After any meeting or call, extract the insights and build an opportunity map.

Two modes:
1. **PRE-CALL** → generates the discovery questions to ask
2. **POST-CALL** → turns Annia's notes/transcript into an opportunity map + next action

## How to invoke
```
/interviewer pre | [describe who you're meeting and what you know]
/interviewer post | [paste your notes or transcript from the call]
```

Examples:
- `/interviewer pre | expired listing seller in Deltona, 3/2 pool home, listed 6 months ago, price $315K, didn't sell`
- `/interviewer pre | investor looking for Central FL deals, cash buyer, wants duplex or SFR under $300K`
- `/interviewer pre | buyer couple, moving from Puerto Rico, budget $350K, want good schools, Kissimmee area`
- `/interviewer post | [paste call notes]`

## PRE-CALL MODE

### Output: Discovery Question Set

**GOAL QUESTIONS** (understand what they actually want, not what they say they want)
- "If this home sold in the next 60 days for the right price, what would you do next?"
- "What happened that made you decide NOT to relist right away?"
- "What would have to be different this time for you to feel confident about listing?"

**STORY QUESTIONS** (Teresa Torres' "Excavate the Story" method)
- "Tell me about the day you decided to take it off the market. Walk me through it."
- "What were you expecting at listing? What was the gap?"
- "What conversations did you have with your last agent that you didn't love?"

**ASSUMPTION SURFACING QUESTIONS** (find what they believe that may not be true)
- "What do you think the home is worth today vs. what you listed it at?"
- "Have you seen what comparable homes in your area sold for in the last 90 days?"
- "What's your timeline — is there a hard date you need to close by?"

**PAIN QUESTIONS** (find the real motivation)
- "What's costing you money every month this home sits unsold?"
- "Who else is counting on this sale — is there another property, a move, a life change tied to this?"

**TRUST QUESTIONS** (earn the listing)
- "What would a real estate agent have to show you to earn your trust after your last experience?"
- "What matters more to you — getting the highest price or selling in the shortest time?"

[3-5 questions specific to the situation described]
